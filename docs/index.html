Mandatory Local Ground-Truth Assurance

NASA satellite observations represent broad-area gridded conditions (0.5° × 0.625° for POWER, 9 km for SMAP, 10 km for IMERG), not sub-field sensor truth. AgroShift makes no guaranteed yield, revenue, or water savings claims. All recommendations are explainable, tentative decision-support guidance. Farmers must always verify canal water warabandi schedules, local seed varieties, and field salinity with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa agricultural extension officers before planting.
